# FleetGuard AI — Signal catalogue

Source schema **3.2**, generator **0.3.0**, feature schema **2.0**.

Read alongside the [system definition](domain_specification.md), [data contract](data_contract.md),
[anomaly catalogue](anomaly_catalogue.md) and [feature rules](hierarchical_feature_rules.md).

## Ownership and reporting

One wagon has two bogies, four axles/wheelsets and eight wheels. One battery and
one controller are shared. Four axle generators provide the synthetic moving
power source. BPP, AR and SR are shared wagon signals; BCP is one reading per bogie.
Components provide measurements; the controller combines and reports them.
All readings here are generated by FleetGuard, not taken from railway hardware.

The controller also reports wagon context such as location and motion. Their
inclusion in a report does not mean its processor physically measures every signal.

Ranges below are inclusive contract boundaries, not normal operating bands,
warning thresholds or certified railway limits. Nullable measurements require
quality metadata. The full record schemas also contain identities and lineage.

## Wagon and shared systems

Nested source path: `event root`.

| Field | Unit / role | Allowed contract values | Meaning and healthy generation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `travel_direction` | state | `forward`, `reverse` | Forward or reverse along the route fixture. Seeded journey choice. |
| `operating_state` | state | `stationary`, `moving`, `braking` | Stationary, moving or braking. Planned operating context, preserved through fault injection. |
| `journey_phase` | state | `origin_dwell`, `running`, `intermediate_dwell`, `destination_dwell` | Origin dwell, running, intermediate dwell or destination dwell. Journey schedule. |
| `route_progress` | fraction | 0 to 1 | Progress along the planned duty; includes dwell. Journey progression, not a distance sensor. |
| `latitude` | degrees | -90 to 90 | North/south position. Interpolated on the coarse route; holds during dwell. |
| `longitude` | degrees | -180 to 180 | East/west position. Interpolated with latitude as one coordinate pair. |
| `speed_kph` | km/h | 0 to 120 | Wagon motion speed. Planned acceleration, running, braking and dwell. |
| `ambient_temp_c` | °C | -40 to 50 | Outside temperature. Time-dependent baseline with seeded variation. |
| `longitudinal_acceleration_mps2` | m/s² | -5 to 5 | Acceleration along the wagon. Derived from speed changes and bounded variation. |
| `lateral_acceleration_mps2` | m/s² | -5 to 5 | Side-to-side acceleration. Synthetic bounded variation; not calibrated curve dynamics. |
| `vertical_acceleration_mps2` | m/s² | -10 to 10 | Vertical acceleration. Synthetic bounded variation; not a sampled impact waveform. |
| `rail_condition` | state | `dry`, `wet`, `leaf_contaminated`, `icy` | Dry, wet, leaf-contaminated or icy context. Synthetic condition varies with route progress and temperature. |
| `estimated_adhesion_coefficient` | dimensionless | 0 to 0.6 | Reported estimate of wheel–rail grip. Rail-condition-dependent internal grip plus estimation noise. |
| `brake_pipe_pressure_bar` | bar | 0 to 6; null with quality | Shared brake-pipe pressure (BPP). Falls during simulated application; recovered during release. |
| `auxiliary_reservoir_pressure_bar` | bar | 0 to 6; null with quality | Shared auxiliary reservoir pressure (AR). Simulated reservoir response with bounded noise. |
| `secondary_reservoir_pressure_bar` | bar | 0 to 6; null with quality | Shared secondary reservoir pressure (SR). Simulated reservoir response with bounded noise. |
| `battery_voltage_v` | V | 3.0 to 4.2 | One shared synthetic cell-equivalent battery voltage. Starts full; charges in generator mode, discharges in battery mode. |
| `power_source` | state | `axle_generators`, `battery` | Axle generation or battery power. Healthy generator mode at speed ≥5 km/h; battery otherwise. |
| `available_axle_generators` | count | 0 to 4 | Number of available axle generators. Four healthy; generator failure reduces availability. |
| `brake_demand` | state | `apply`, `release` | Independent simulated apply/release command. Context from planned operations; not inferred from faulty pressures. |

## Controller

Nested source path: `controller`.

| Field | Unit / role | Allowed contract values | Meaning and healthy generation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `temperature_c` | °C | -40 to 100 | Controller internal temperature. Outside temperature, speed contribution and small seeded variation. |

## Wheel metadata and other static asset inputs

`diameter_mm` is 800–1100 mm, stored per left/right wheel in asset metadata.
Healthy left/right difference is at most 2 mm; both share axle rotation.
Normalised wheel rows carry diameter beside bearing temperature. Other asset
inputs are fleet ID, age, nominal load, temperature/vibration/pressure baselines,
battery standby days and generator seed. They configure the simulation; they
are not new measurements at every timestamp. Full fields are in the JSON Schemas.

## How these signals become features

| Information | Current treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Numerical readings | Valid-only mean, minimum, maximum, first and last; quality counts |
| States | Latest, counts, sample fractions and observed transitions |
| Latitude / longitude | Last observed coordinate pair plus its observation time |
| Controller counters | Numerical summaries plus observed positive changes and decrease count |
| Known attributes | Handbrake flag and wheel diameter retained, checked for agreement |
| Component IDs and timestamps | Grouping and traceability; not proposed predictive measurements |
| Truth and scenario parameters | Evaluation context only; never model inputs |

Wagon features flatten controller fields with a `controller_` prefix. Bogie
features also carry shared wagon speed, adhesion and pneumatic context. Axle
features retain axle measurements; wheel features retain the individual bearing
measurement. There are **28 numerical placements** across the four feature levels,
including repeated context, not 28 distinct sensors. See the complete field
mapping in [hierarchical feature rules](hierarchical_feature_rules.md).

## Missing versus silent

Null plus `missing`/`invalid` means an unavailable reading in a received report.
A locked axle instead reports valid zero wheelset speed. A controller outage
produces no report at all, so no nested component readings exist for that slot.
Synthetic cause labels live in separate truth files. Never replace either form
of absence with a physical zero or forward-fill silently through an outage.
